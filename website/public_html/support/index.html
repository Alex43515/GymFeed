How can we help?

GymFeed support

Contact GymFeed

For account, subscription, safety, privacy or technical questions, email us. Include your GymFeed username and a short description of the problem, but never send your password.

Email official@gymfeed.io

Account deletion

You can request deletion through the app’s profile settings. If you cannot access your account, email official@gymfeed.io from the address associated with your account.

Subscriptions

Google Play subscriptions can be managed from Google Play’s Payments & subscriptions area. Include the purchase platform and order reference when contacting us, but do not email payment-card information.

Safety

Use the in-app report and block controls for unwanted content or behavior. For an urgent threat, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
